**Notes for tester review:**

- TC-001–TC-002 (Critical), TC-003–TC-004 (High), TC-005–TC-009 (Medium) map 1:1 to R-01 through R-06 and the stock-boundary scenario, consistent with the scenarios artifact.
- **TC-009's risk band is flagged, not confirmed.** The scenario draft noted this boundary case exercises the stock-threshold (0 vs 1) but not the concurrency race condition that gives R-02 its critical rating — it was tagged `@risk-medium` by default rather than inheriting R-02's critical band. Please confirm whether Medium is correct or whether this should be escalated.
- TC-006/TC-007/TC-008 are kept as three separate cases rather than one combined case: each exercises a distinct equivalence class (SQL-injection pattern, script-injection pattern, length-boundary input) with a different expected message, so collapsing them would hide a materially different expected result — this follows the "one sharp example per condition" principle rather than volume for its own sake.
- Negative-case coverage per functional area: promo/discount (TC-002 stock rejection, TC-006–TC-008 injection), payment authorization (TC-004 decline), reliability (TC-003 duplicate-charge prevention) — satisfying the ≥1 negative case per functional area requirement.
- 3DS/OTP scope, stock-vs-authorization ordering, and client-side promo bypass remain out of scope here as they were not present as scenarios (marked TBD upstream) — no test cases have been fabricated for them.
- Execution status for all cases is "Pending"; pass/fail evidence must be recorded from actual sandbox runs, not asserted here.
